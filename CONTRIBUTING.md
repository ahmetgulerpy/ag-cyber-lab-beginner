# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in improving AG CYBER LAB Beginner.

Contributions, suggestions, documentation improvements, and educational content are welcome.

Before submitting a contribution, please follow these guidelines:

## Contribution Guidelines

1. **Explain the purpose:** Describe the educational problem your change solves and why it is useful.
2. **Identify the affected module:** Keep module-specific changes within the relevant module whenever possible.
3. **Preserve lab safety:** Do not introduce real-world scanning, exploitation, credential collection, or destructive behavior into simulated exercises without explicit maintainer review.
4. **Protect sensitive information:** Never commit `.env` files, credentials, API tokens, personal data, or third-party confidential information.
5. **Exclude generated files:** Do not commit `node_modules`, build output, or unnecessary generated artifacts.
6. **Provide testing details:** Include clear reproduction steps and explain how your changes were tested.
7. **Maintain documentation quality:** Preserve Turkish educational terminology and update both English and Turkish repository-level documentation when relevant.

## Licensing and Contributions

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that you have the right to submit the contributed material.

Submitted contributions are subject to the repository's existing license unless a separate written agreement is established with the project maintainer.

Submitting a contribution does not independently grant permission to change the project's license or redistribute the repository under different licensing terms.

## Questions

For questions about contributions or proposed changes, contact the project maintainer through the official AG CYBER LAB communication channel.
